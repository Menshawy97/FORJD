Train

Programs, templates and a workout that runs offline.

Start a suggested session, follow a multi-week program, or build your own from the exercise library. Every workout you finish becomes history you can compare against next time.

  • Exercise library with muscle groups and equipment
  • Your own custom exercises and routines
  • Strength, hypertrophy and conditioning blocks
  • Works with no signal, syncs later
Run

Runs live in the same history as your lifts.

Track a run with pace, distance and heart rate. It counts toward the same week as your gym sessions, so your training load tells the whole story.

  • Live pace, distance and time
  • Heart rate from your watch
  • HYROX-style sessions that mix running and stations
Recover

Your wearable's data, with the source kept.

Connect Apple Health, Health Connect or WHOOP. If two devices both record your sleep, FORJD keeps both readings and shows you which one it's using. It never quietly overwrites one with the other.

  • Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and steps
  • A daily readiness score on your home screen
  • FORJD Insights: short, plain notes built from your own numbers
Body

InBody scans that finally mean something.

Add each InBody result and FORJD keeps the full history: weight, body fat, skeletal muscle mass and the segmental breakdown, charted against the training that produced it.

  • Scan history with change since last time
  • Check every value before it's saved
  • Trends for each metric, month by month
Eat

Calories and macros without a second app.

Log meals by breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner, set your own targets, and save the meals you eat every week so logging them takes one tap.

  • Protein, carbs and fat against your goals
  • Saved meals for the things you eat often
  • Search foods or add your own
Compete

A leaderboard for the city you actually train in.

Your city board ranks you on activity, with strength, running and HYROX boards to follow. Boards only appear once enough people in a city have joined, so a rank always means something.

  • This month and all-time rankings
  • Tap through to any athlete's public profile
  • Choose what your profile shows in privacy settings

Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro renews automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel from your App Store or Google Play subscriptions at any time.

Which phones does FORJD work on?

iPhone and Android. On iPhone it reads from Apple Health; on Android it reads from Health Connect.

Which wearables can I connect?

Anything that writes to Apple Health or Health Connect, plus WHOOP directly.

Do I need an InBody scanner?

No. Body composition tracking is there if you get scanned at a gym or clinic, but everything else works without it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. You can export or delete your data from the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, username, and the profile details you choose to add, such as your city.
  • Training: the workouts, sets, runs and programs you log in FORJD.
  • Health readings you connect: sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and steps from Apple Health, Health Connect or WHOOP, only after you give permission on your phone.
  • Body composition: the InBody results you add.
  • Nutrition: the foods and meals you log.

How we use it

To run the features you see: your history, charts, readiness score, FORJD Insights and, if you opt in, your place on your city's leaderboard. Each health reading is stored with the app or device it came from, so you can always see where a number originated.

What we never do

Health data never reaches an analytics or advertising service. Not in the app, not on our servers, not while we're fixing a bug. We don't sell it, rent it or use it to target ads.

Data read from Apple Health and Health Connect is used only to provide FORJD's health and fitness features, as both platforms require.

Your controls

  • Export: download a copy of your account data from the app.
  • Delete: delete your account and its data from the app.
  • Disconnect: revoke Apple Health, Health Connect or WHOOP access at any time.
  • Public profile: choose what other athletes can see in privacy settings.

Data sources

City names and locations come from GeoNames (geonames.org), used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
