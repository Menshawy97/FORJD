### L2-1 — CORS reflects any origin when `ALLOWED_WEB_ORIGINS` is unset
- **Severity:** low · **Where:** `apps/api/src/cors.ts:26-42`
- **Scenario:** safe today (bearer tokens, `credentials` off, pinned by `cors.spec.ts`); becomes a credential-theft vector only if cookie auth or `credentials: true` is added later.
- **Known gap?** yes — the 2026-09-10 audit's LOW item, unchanged.

### L2-2 — Forgot-password / resend-confirmation throttled per IP only
- **Severity:** low · **Where:** `apps/api/src/auth/auth.controller.ts:75-95`
- **Scenario:** rotating IPs can send many reset/confirmation emails to one victim; Supabase's per-address limit is the documented backstop.
- **Suggested fix:** a per-email counter, or document reliance on Supabase's limit.

### L2-3 — WHOOP OAuth `state` may not be invalidated after use (unconfirmed)
- **Severity:** low · **Where:** `apps/api/src/integrations/whoop/whoop.controller.ts:87-96`, `whoop-callback.service.ts` (not fully read)
- **Suggested fix:** confirm `completeAuthorization` clears the pending state on success and failure. WHOOP codes are single-use, which mitigates it.
