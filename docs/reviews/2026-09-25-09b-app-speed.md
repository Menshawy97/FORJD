# Lane 9b — App speed: screens, start-up and data loading (2026-09-25, at b1f006a)

### L9b-1 — Progress loads its four sections one after another
- **Severity:** high · **Where:** `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/progress.tsx:58-86`
- **Scenario:** `getProgressStrength`, `getBodyScanSeries`, `getHealthObservationSeries`, `getReadiness` are each awaited in turn; with every request crossing us-central1 ↔ eu-west-1, first-visit time is ~4× what it needs to be. The file's own docblock claims to mirror Home, which uses `Promise.allSettled` (`index.tsx:114-122`).
- **Rule / ADR:** speed budget "first visit under 1 s".
- **Suggested fix:** one `Promise.allSettled`, each section committed from its own result.

### L9b-2 — Train's previous-workout card reads each exercise from SQLite one at a time
- **Severity:** medium · **Where:** `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/train.tsx:79-98`
- **Scenario:** on every visit: `listWorkoutSessions` → `getWorkoutSession` → N sequential `getCachedExercise` awaits.
- **Suggested fix:** `Promise.all` over the exercises.

### L9b-3 — `getMe()` fetched separately by the root layout, Home and Profile
- **Severity:** medium · **Where:** `apps/mobile/src/app/_layout.tsx:227`, `(tabs)/index.tsx:116`, `(tabs)/profile.tsx:264`
- **Scenario:** three identical cross-Atlantic requests in one launch; no shared cache or in-flight de-duplication.
- **Suggested fix:** one shared `getMe` cache (short TTL, invalidated on profile writes).

### L9b-4 — Home, Train and Rank refetch everything on every tab focus
- **Severity:** medium · **Where:** `(tabs)/index.tsx:171-178`, `(tabs)/train.tsx:118-154`, `(tabs)/rank.tsx:69-76`
- **Scenario:** no staleness check; flicking between tabs re-issues the full request set each time. No spinner on revisit today only because tab screens stay mounted.
- **Suggested fix:** `lastLoadedAt` per screen with a short minimum interval, or one small shared cache utility.

### L9b-5 — Train's "My programs" section loads once and never refreshes
- **Severity:** low (staleness) · **Where:** `(tabs)/train.tsx:173-194`
- **Scenario:** create a program, go back to Train: the section is stale until the app restarts.
- **Suggested fix:** load it in the focus effect like the rest of the screen.

## Start-up
Splash waits only for the font and one SecureStore read; `getMe` and the upload drain are fire-and-forget. The brand intro is drawn over an already-mounted screen. SQLite, Health Connect and notifications are not awaited at the root. Meets the cold-start budget by construction (not timed on a device).

## Bundle
`npx expo export --platform android`: Hermes bytecode **5.8 MB**, 2,298 modules. Largest assets are fonts: `MaterialSymbols_400Regular.ttf` 963 KB (usage unconfirmed; the app draws icons as inline SVG) and `Archivo-Variable.ttf` 659 KB. No lodash/moment, no statically bundled JSON (catalogue and foods come from the server into SQLite).

## Checked and clean
H14 (conditional catalogue fetch) and H15 (1 Hz timers) hold; Library uses `SectionList` with `getItemLayout` and a stable `renderItem`; leaderboard rows are paged server-side; other `ScrollView`+`map` lists are small and bounded; food search debounced; nutrition food lookups deduplicated and parallel; timers wall-clock based.

## Unconfirmed
Whether the 963 KB Material Symbols font is used at all; whether `live.tsx`'s whole-screen 1 Hz re-render costs anything measurable on a device.

## Not reached
